Operators:-

    Operators are symbols that are used to perform operations on variables and values.
    Operators are used to assign values to variables, add, subtract, multiply, divide and many more.

    Types of Operators:-
        Arithmetic Operators
        Assignment Operators
        Comparison Operators
        Logical Operators
        Bitwise Operators
        Ternary Operators
        Other Operators

    Arithmetic Operators:-
        +, -, *, /, %, ++, --

    Assignment Operators:-
        =, +=, -=, *=, /=, %=

    Comparison Operators:-
        ==, !=, >, <, >=, <=

    Logical Operators:-
        &&, ||, !

    Bitwise Operators:-
        &, |, ^, ~, <<, >>

    Ternary Operators:-
        ?: (conditional operator)

    Other Operators:-
        sizeof, typeof
